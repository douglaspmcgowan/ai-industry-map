Model Hosting / Inference

Providers that run open-weights (Llama, Qwen, DeepSeek, Mistral) and custom models as managed APIs. The frontier labs host their own models; this category hosts everyone else's. Split into general-purpose, cold-start-specialist, and custom-silicon providers.

Replicate

Replicate · founded 2019
General-purposeCog

What it is. "One API for every open-source model." Huge catalog (text, image, video, audio, custom). Cog (their container format) is the standard way to package models for deployment.

What sets it apart. Breadth of models — if it's on Hugging Face, it's probably on Replicate. Pay-per-prediction pricing.

Connections. Cog is open-source. Broad use among creators / hobbyists for image + video models.

Usage
Hundreds of thousands of developers; strong creator community
Niche
Widest model catalog, creator-friendly
replicate.com

Together AI

Together · CEO Vipul Ved Prakash
LLM inferenceTraining too

What it is. Fast inference for open-weights LLMs (Llama, Qwen, DeepSeek, Mistral). Also offers fine-tuning, dedicated endpoints, and training.

What sets it apart. Competitive pricing, open model catalog, FlashAttention-like custom optimizations. Popular drop-in replacement for people who want Llama-class quality without hosting it themselves.

Connections. Funded by Nvidia, Salesforce Ventures. Co-published RedPajama open-dataset work.

Usage
Hundreds of millions of API calls per month; growing fast
Niche
Best combo of pricing + open-model breadth
together.ai

Fireworks AI

Fireworks · founded by ex-Meta PyTorch leads
Fast inference

What it is. LLM inference with strong focus on latency + throughput. Popular for production Llama / Qwen / DeepSeek workloads. Also offers fine-tuning (LoRA + full) and custom deployments.

What sets it apart. Founded by former Meta PyTorch team → deep kernel optimization expertise. Popular with enterprises needing predictable p99 latency.

Connections. Tight partnerships with Meta (Llama) and Nvidia.

Usage
Strong enterprise adoption for LLM inference
Strength
Low-latency production inference
fireworks.ai

Modal

Modal Labs · CEO Erik Bernhardsson (ex-Spotify, author of luigi + annoy)
Serverless GPUs

What it is. Serverless cloud for Python + ML. Decorate a function with `@app.function(gpu="A100")` and it runs on a remote GPU on demand. Great for custom model serving + batch inference.

What sets it apart. Cold starts under a second even for GPU containers — famously hard problem solved elegantly. Python-native developer experience. Popular for one-off ML jobs + custom model endpoints.

Connections. Spotify heritage via Bernhardsson. Popular with research teams and indie ML developers.

Usage
Tens of thousands of developers; beloved in Python ML circles
Niche
Custom model endpoints + Python batch jobs
modal.com

Baseten

Baseten · founded 2019
Custom deployments

What it is. Managed model serving for custom / fine-tuned models. "Truss" is their open-source packaging format. Focus: reliable production deployments with good observability.

What sets it apart. Strong story for enterprises with their own trained models who want managed infra without building it. Sits between Replicate (hobbyist) and the hyperscalers.

Connections. Used by Writer, Descript, Patreon for ML serving.

Usage
Mid-market and enterprise ML teams
Niche
Custom-model production serving with hand-holding
baseten.co

Groq

Groq · founded 2016 by Jonathan Ross (ex-Google TPU)
Custom siliconUltra-fast

What it is. Inference on Groq's custom LPU (Language Processing Unit) chips. Delivers the fastest tokens-per-second for open-weights LLMs available anywhere.

What sets it apart. Hundreds of tokens/second on Llama 70B-class models — demos feel instant, like reading a file. Founder invented the Google TPU.

Connections. Often confused with Elon Musk's xAI Grok. Has a Saudi sovereign deal for regional deployment.

Usage
Popular for real-time voice agents + interactive UIs
Niche
Lowest-latency inference in the industry
groq.com

Cerebras

Cerebras · founded 2016 · CEO Andrew Feldman
Custom siliconWafer-scale

What it is. Custom wafer-scale chip (a single chip the size of a dinner plate). Runs inference on open-weights LLMs at speeds competitive with Groq.

What sets it apart. Only company shipping wafer-scale compute. Used by Glean, Meta, and the US national labs for high-throughput inference + training. Filed for IPO in 2024.

Connections. Rivalry with Groq for "fastest inference" mindshare. Different tech bet (wafer-scale vs. custom SRAM LPU).

Usage
Larger enterprise contracts than Groq on average; smaller hobbyist mindshare
Niche
Enterprise-scale fast inference + training
cerebras.net

RunPod

RunPod
Raw GPU rental

What it is. Raw GPU cloud — rent H100s, A100s, L40s by the hour for training, fine-tuning, or custom inference. Also offers serverless GPU mode.

What sets it apart. Cheapest GPU hour prices of any "real" cloud. Two-tier: "Community Cloud" (cheaper, less reliable) vs "Secure Cloud" (enterprise SLA). Huge with indie ML researchers.

Connections. Alternative to Vast.ai, Lambda Labs, Paperspace.

Usage
Massive indie + research adoption; the "cheap GPU" default
Niche
Raw GPU rental / training on a budget
runpod.io
